# Summary
The live browser check of task #61 measured all 12 d4 numbers on the real label atlas. The three digit-1 labels sit 0.2553 mm (fringe-inclusive) / 0.2757 mm (solid ink) from the flat-triangle border, above the acceptance upper bound of 0.25 mm; the other nine numbers pass (0.1507 to 0.2464 mm). The need (flush numbers, no ink in the rounded band) is met visually; the acceptance range and spec 2 are in tension for the digit 1 and a user decision is needed.

# Specs
Evidence (Chromium, live page importing the served app modules, 1024 px atlas, 0.0408 mm/px, alpha>0 ink = the developer's recorded convention):
- Per-number smallest ink-to-flat-border distance, solid ink (alpha>=128) in parentheses: digit 1 on faces 0/1/2: 0.2553 (0.2757); digit 4: 0.1507-0.1593 (0.1797-0.1860); digit 2: 0.2050-0.2165 (0.2403-0.2464); digit 3: 0.1961-0.2072 (0.2314-0.2403). All 12 in mm.
- Acceptance range [0.10, 0.25] mm: 9/12 pass. The three digit-1 labels exceed the upper bound by 0.0053 mm (0.13 atlas px) fringe-inclusive, 0.0257 mm (0.63 atlas px) solid.
- Global worst clearance 0.1507 mm (digit 4, face 2) is inside spec 2's 0.12-0.22 window, so per spec 2 no constant change is due; D4_LABEL_CENTER = 0.305 confirmed live.
- inkOutside = 0 (no ink pixel on or outside the flat triangle, both ink criteria). Synthetic self-check: square with its edge 0.5 mm inside a border measured 0.5338 mm.
- Contradicts the spec 1 fact "calculated clearance of the real outline is 0.15 to 0.21 mm": the measured solid range is 0.1797-0.2757 mm; the digit 1 glyph ink lands higher in its box than the calculation assumed.
- Visual impact is negligible: at dpr 2 the solid gap of the digit 1 is about 4.8 device px (about 2.4 css px) versus 3.1 px for the digit 4; in the rendered crops the 1s look marginally less flush but fine. Screenshots: /tmp/dice61-lt/out/d4-atlas-cell-zoom.png (atlas cell with red flat-triangle border), /tmp/dice61-lt/out/scene-die-zoom-0.png and -1.png (rendered dice), /tmp/dice61-lt/out/results-atlas.json.
Fix direction: either raise the acceptance/spec upper bound to about 0.28-0.30 mm (need still met, no code change), or nudge only the digit-1 placement per digit (conflicts with the spec 2/scope rule of changing only D4_LABEL_CENTER and keeping the size).
